About Us

Simplifying Trade, Empowering Commerce

The National Single Window (NSW) is a cross-government website that facilitates trade by giving all parties involved in trade and transport access to a single point to lodge standardized information and documents with a single entry point to fulfill all import, export, and transit-related regulatory requirements.
